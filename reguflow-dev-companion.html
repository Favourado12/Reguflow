🏛️ CAC (Corporate Affairs Commission)

Company Registration

ReguFlow implements a comprehensive CAC registration workflow following CAMA 2020 requirements.

  • Name Reservation: Integrated with CAC Public Search portal (search.cac.gov.ng)
  • Business Types: Supports Private Limited (LTD), Public Limited (PLC), Limited by Guarantee (LTD/GTE), and Unlimited (ULTD)
  • Director Requirements: Collects NIN, International Passport, or Driver's License
  • Document Upload: Signature specimens and ID verification
  • Fee Structure: ₦500 for name reservation

Post-Incorporation Compliance

Annual returns and Persons with Significant Control (PSC) filings are mandatory for Nigerian entities.

💰 Share Capital Requirements

Minimum Capital by Entity Type

  • Mobile Money Operators (MMO): ₦2,000,000,000 (₦2 Billion)
  • Payment Service Solution Providers (PSSP): ₦100,000,000 (₦100 Million)
  • Super Agents: ₦50,000,000 (₦50 Million)

These requirements represent significant barriers to entry for fintech startups and are enforced by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN).

🔒 AML (Anti-Money Laundering)

2026 Automated AML Enforcement

Deadline: June 10, 2026

  • Real-time Transaction Monitoring: All PSSPs, MMOs, and Digital Banks must implement automated monitoring systems
  • Biometric Liveness KYC: Integration of biometric verification APIs required
  • Transaction Logging: Comprehensive audit trails for all financial transactions
  • Suspicious Activity Reporting: Automated flagging and reporting mechanisms

⚖️ FCCPC (Federal Competition & Consumer Protection Commission)

Digital Lending Guidelines

Unregistered digital lenders face penalties of ₦100,000,000+ under 2026 enforcement logic.

  • Registration Requirement: All digital lending platforms must be registered with FCCPC
  • Consumer Protection: Transparent terms, fair interest rates, and ethical collection practices
  • Data Privacy: Compliance with NDPR (Nigeria Data Protection Regulation)
  • Penalties: Severe fines for non-compliance, including operational shutdowns

🤖 AI Governance (NITDA Framework)

2026 AI Ethical Framework

All AI agents operating in the Nigerian financial sector must implement "Storage Guard" protocols.

  • Storage Guard Protocol: Prevents autonomous deletion of transaction logs or user data
  • Explainability: AI decisions must be traceable and explainable
  • Human-in-the-Loop (HITL): Critical actions require human approval
  • Risk Mitigation: Comprehensive risk assessment for AI systems
  • Liability: Entities are strictly liable for autonomous agent misbehavior
